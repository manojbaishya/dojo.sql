Which employees are responsible for the ten highest individual sales?

```sql
SELECT 
    e.EmployeeId AS [Employee Id],
    e.FirstName || ' ' || e.LastName AS [Employee Name],
    c.CustomerId AS [Customer Id],
    c.FirstName || ' ' || c.LastName AS [Customer Name],
    i.Total AS Amount
FROM 
    Invoice AS i 
INNER JOIN
    Customer AS c 
        ON c.CustomerId = i.CustomerId
INNER JOIN
    Employee AS e
        ON e.EmployeeId = c.SupportRepId
ORDER BY
    i.Total DESC
LIMIT 10 OFFSET 0;
```
