Year - Month - Date - Hour - Minute - Second

[https://www.sqlite.org/lang_datefunc.html](https://www.sqlite.org/lang_datefunc.html)

[https://www.w3.org/TR/NOTE-datetime](https://www.w3.org/TR/NOTE-datetime)

```sql

SELECT 
    e.FirstName,
    e.LastName,
    STRFTIME('%Y-%m-%d', e.BirthDate) AS [Birthday],
    STRFTIME('%Y-%m-%d', 'now') - STRFTIME('%Y-%m-%d', e.BirthDate) AS [Age]
FROM Employee AS e
ORDER BY [Age] ASC

```
