Question: How many customers purchased two songs at $0.99 each?

Approach: 

Customer purchases are logged in Invoices.

Each Invoice can contain many invoice lines -- Invoice: one to many : Invoice lines
Each invoice has total column for sum of all invoice lines' price


```sql
SELECT
	c.FirstName || " " || c.LastName as Name,
	i.BillingAddress as Address,
	i.BillingCity as City,
	i.Total as Amount
FROM Invoice as i 
INNER JOIN Customer as c 
	ON c.CustomerId = i.CustomerId 
WHERE
	i.Total = 0.99 * 2
ORDER BY
    i.InvoiceDate;
```
