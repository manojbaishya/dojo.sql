WHERE clause comes after the FROM clause and before the GROUP BY clause

```sql
SELECT 
    i.BillingCity AS [Billing City],
    ROUND(AVG(i.Total), 2) AS [Average Purchase] 
FROM 
    Invoice AS i
WHERE 
    [Billing City] LIKE 'L%'
    OR [Billing City] LIKE 'l%'
GROUP BY 
    [Billing City]
ORDER BY 
    [Average Purchase] DESC;
```
