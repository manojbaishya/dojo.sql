Q. Find invoices between $1.8 to $5

A.

```sql
SELECT
    c.FirstName || " " || c.LastName as Name,
    i.BillingAddress as Address,
    i.BillingCity as City,
    i.Total as Amount
FROM Invoice as i 
INNER JOIN Customer as c 
    ON c.CustomerId = i.CustomerId 
WHERE
    i.Total BETWEEN 1.98 AND 5
ORDER BY
    i.InvoiceDate;
```


Q. Find invoices which are totalled $1.8 or $3.96 (Tuples)

A.

```sql
SELECT
    c.FirstName || " " || c.LastName as Name,
    i.BillingAddress as Address,
    i.BillingCity as City,
    i.Total as Amount
FROM Invoice as i 
INNER JOIN Customer as c 
    ON c.CustomerId = i.CustomerId 
WHERE
    i.Total IN (1.98, 3.96)
ORDER BY
    i.InvoiceDate;
```
