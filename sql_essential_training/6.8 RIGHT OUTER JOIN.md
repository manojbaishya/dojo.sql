### Mirror Image of LEFT OUTER JOIN

```sql
SELECT
    i.CustomerId AS [Customer Id],
    c.FirstName || ' ' || c.LastName AS [Customer Name],
    i.InvoiceId AS [Invoice Id],
    i.InvoiceDate AS [Purchase Date],
    i.Total AS Amount
FROM
    Invoice as i
RIGHT OUTER JOIN Customer as c ON
    i.CustomerId = c.CustomerId
-- WHERE c.CustomerId IS NULL
ORDER BY
    c.CustomerId ASC,
    i.InvoiceDate ASC
LIMIT 100 OFFSET 0;
```
